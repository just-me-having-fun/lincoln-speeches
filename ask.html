Correspondence · Washington, spring 1865

Ask Mr. Lincoln

What this is. A machine correspondent instructed to answer as Lincoln answered — in his plain register, grounded in the six speeches on this site and in his known record through March 4, 1865. It is an experiment, not a séance: it will be wrong sometimes, and it cannot know anything that came after its date. Fifteen questions to the hour, per caller.

Replies are machine-written in period style and may be inaccurate. No history of your letters is kept beyond the present conversation.
