The Lincoln Speeches

1838 1858 1860 1861 1863 1865

Six speeches, chosen not because they are the most quoted but because together they trace a single arc: a lawyer in his twenties warning against mob rule, and — twenty-seven years later — a president with five weeks to live asking his country for malice toward none.

Every text below is transcribed in full from archival sources and carries its citation. Lincoln’s words entered the public domain long ago; nothing here has been abridged, paraphrased, or “modernized.”
